# Landing page brief

## 1. Goal
Turn visitors into WhatsApp/contact inquiries for (a) a free consultation about a custom project, (b) a ready-made template for their niche,
(c) a waitlist for niches that are not live yet. Secondary: be found by niche searches (see `docs/niche-pages.md`).

## 2. Audience (pick the language and examples that fit each)
- Local service businesses (salon, barbershop, workshop, rental): want online booking, deposits, reminders.
- Online sellers and resellers: store, digital products, top-up and PPOB, reseller pricing.
- Creators and educators: courses, membership, community.
- Small companies and professionals: credible website, portfolio, leads.
- Startups and operators with a unique idea: custom MVP, marketplace.
- Agencies: white-label.

## 3. Positioning
One line (draft): "Website dan software yang siap pakai, bisa disesuaikan, dan dirawat oleh satu tim."
English: "Ready-made, customizable websites and software, maintained by one team."
Core message: start from a working product instead of a blank page; pay for what you need; add features as you grow;
look unique through your own theme and branding; one team keeps it updated, secure, and supported.
How it works (internally): one shared platform, nine product "engines", many niches. The page sells the niches, not the engines.

## 5. Page structure (adapt to the existing layout)
1. Hero: headline, one-sentence subhead, primary CTA (WhatsApp), secondary CTA (see niches).
2. Who it is for: 4-6 niche cards linking to niche pages or the waitlist.
3. Why this approach: 4 value points (start from a working product; pay for what you need; unique look via theme; one team maintains it).
4. How it works: 4 steps (consultation -> choose niche/template or custom scope -> setup and launch -> care plan).
5. Two ways to work with us: ready-made template vs custom project (plus care plan). No prices unless provided.
6. Niche grid: the niche list with availability badges.
7. Proof: portfolio, testimonials, logos. PLACEHOLDERS ONLY until real material is provided.
8. FAQ: 6-8 questions (see copy deck). Answers must match confirmed policies only.
9. Final CTA: WhatsApp + contact form. Footer: legal pages, contact, social.

## 6. Tone
Clear, friendly, professional, concrete. Short sentences. Avoid hype and jargon ("monolit modular" is internal, not for visitors).
Talk about outcomes for the visitor's business, not our architecture.

## 7. Tracking (only if analytics already exist on the page)
Events: click WhatsApp (by section), submit contact/waitlist form, click niche card, click demo link.

## 8. Information needed from the owner (list what is missing in your summary)
WhatsApp number; real portfolio items; real testimonials; demo site URLs; which offers are live; whether to show prices; legal pages
(privacy policy, terms); logo and brand colours if not in the repo; preferred languages.

## 9. Success checks
Page loads fast on mobile; one clear primary CTA per screen; every claim is true today; every placeholder is listed;
Lighthouse performance/accessibility/SEO scores not lower than before.
