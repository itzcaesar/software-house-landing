# Business context (for accuracy of copy)

## What we sell
1. Custom projects: software and websites built to order on the same internal platform, so delivery is faster and cheaper than from scratch.
2. Ready-made templates by niche: an engine + a theme, configured and launched for the client. Differences between niches are configuration.
3. Care plans (monthly): hosting, updates, security patches, backups, monitoring, support.
4. Later: hosted SaaS (business tools, AI workspace).

## Product families (internal) -> niches (what visitors see)
- Company / Portfolio / Directory: company profile, portfolio, business directory, job board.
- Online Store: online shop, dropshipping, reseller, pre-order, ebook and digital products, restaurant ordering, catering.
- Top-Up & PPOB: game top-up, e-wallet top-up, pulsa and data, bill payment.
- Booking & Ticketing: services, salon/barbershop, workshop, car/motorbike rental, events and tickets.
- Learning & Membership: online courses, membership, community/forum.
- Marketplace: multi-seller marketplace, freelancer services, digital assets, online tutoring.
- AI: support chatbot (first), then writing/search/study/document assistants.
- Business Tools: POS, inventory, invoicing (first), later accounting, CRM, HR, payroll, projects.
- Hosting & Server Billing: VPS, game servers.

## Customization levels (use for "can you customize it?" answers)
Branding (colours, fonts, layout) -> configuration (fields, steps, payment methods, notifications) -> add-on modules (reports, API, loyalty, reseller tiers) ->
custom module for unique workflows. Changes that need new development are quoted separately.

## Working process (draft; confirm before publishing as policy)
Discovery call -> demo and scope -> written quote and contract with deposit -> setup and branding -> content and integrations ->
acceptance testing -> launch and training -> warranty period -> care plan.

## Licensing (draft; confirm before publishing)
Client keeps their content and customer data and can export it. We keep the platform, engines and themes; the client receives a licence to run them for their business.
Source-code access and custom-module ownership are agreed in the contract.

## Things the page must not claim
- Guaranteed rankings, sales, or income.
- Being a payment gateway, holding customer funds, or any financial/regulatory licence.
- Compliance guarantees. (Data protection and payments are handled with licensed providers and legal advice.)
- Specific delivery times, prices, warranty length, or SLA unless the owner supplies them.
- Anything marked "Coming soon" being available now.
- Client names, numbers, or results that are not real and approved.
